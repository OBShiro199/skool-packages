# Ethereel: raw talking-head footage to edited, captioned, scheduled shorts
slug `ethereel`, category `content`, art `{ motif: 'waveform', tone: 'violet' }`, status `full`, difficulty 3, buildHours ~45, prefix `eth-`. No price given: compute per-video cost (transcription + Claude + render compute + Remotion licence share + storage + posting) and set plans at 75%+ margin (e.g. Creator / Pro by videos per month), 7-day trial. Maths in costs.notes.

Ethereel is the multi-tenant cloud SaaS version of the owner's local tool "Rosewell Reels" (summary below). Each creator signs up, sets brand settings (series title, handles, hashtags, vocabulary, filter preset, music, posting slots/timezone, follow card), connects TikTok/Instagram through Zernio (Ayrshare as fallback, [verify]), uploads raw clips; the pipeline runs in the cloud:
Supabase Storage (TUS resumable) → Trigger.dev v4 job queue (ffmpeg build extension) → prepare/normalise (CFR, SDR tone-map, 16 kHz audio) → transcribe with word timings (choose Groq Whisper large-v3-turbo vs Deepgram nova-3, with price) → silence map + adaptive threshold + cut list → face/eye detection (MediaPipe Face Landmarker in a task, or Google Vision) → Claude (`claude-opus-5-5`, $4/$20 per MTok) cutaway plan with JSON schema + deterministic layout rules engine → subtitle → follow-card cue → Remotion render on Lambda (1080x1920, 30fps; company licence matters for SaaS) → audio rebuild (10 ms fades, -14 LUFS, music ~25 dB under voice, SFX) → social export spec + validation → Review (TikTok/IG overlay preview, caption Save/Rewrite, Pass/Reject/Request revision with Claude-planned allowed ops, Undo) → Queue (5 daily slots 09:00, 11:00, 14:00, 17:00, 19:00 in creator TZ; Zernio keeps uploads 7 days, so hand over within 6 days; sync every 10 min via pg_cron; cover frame at 3 s; re-render replaces scheduled file).
Principles: human in the loop (nothing posts without Pass); edit.json is the single source of truth per video (jsonb + versioned revisions table); Claude decides content, code decides layout. Super beta (visual-events pipeline) is a later milestone.

Exact parameters to preserve:
- Silence cut: threshold = voice average loudness minus 12 dB; clicks under 60 ms don't break a pause; remove only pauses over 400 ms; keep 120 ms after last word and 100 ms before next; 200 ms lead-in before first word; 400 ms after last word. Word starts snapped to where sound begins, ends to where it stops. Vocabulary priming + spelling fixes.
- Zooms: about one cut in three gets an instant 1.12x punch-in on the eyes, never the opening shot, never twice in a row, never short segments, seeded from the video name. Opening shot slow 1 to 1.06 push-in.
- Colour preset (warmth, sharpness, brightness, contrast, saturation) via ffmpeg, same maths as the browser preview; captions never graded.
- Captions: Inter SemiBold 56 px, tight tracking, white with thin black outline and soft shadow, at 74% frame height; pages of 2 to 4 words, max 1.2 s, break at sentence ends and clauses, single leftover words folded into the next page; appear on the first word, no animation; over a cutaway turn dark ink with no outline on the exact frame; per-word captionOverrides.
- Opening title: series title near top with orange hand-drawn marker scribble drawing on in two strokes with pen-scratch SFX; 2 to 3 word lowercase Claude subtitle below; stop-motion jitter and grain; leaves on first cut after ~3.5 s, or at 5 s.
- Follow card at the cue line ("still watching, follow me"): avatar, handle, Follow button that fills to "Following ✓" with a burst.
- Cutaways: hand-drawn diagram on a maths exercise-book page while voice continues; about one every 16 s, max 4, each 3 to 6.5 s. Kinds: Flow (2 to 4 boxes + arrows), Versus, Number, List (2 to 4 dashed lines), Graph, Image (creator screenshots as tilted prints with white mat). Claude returns per cutaway: start/end, kind, title (≤4 words), items (≤3 words each), beats, punch (circle/strike/underline/arrow + 1 to 3 word note). Rules after Claude: snap to word boundaries; never in first 3 s; ≥6 s apart; no overlap with follow card; text clipped; each asset once. Layout from a rules engine (no collisions, inside margins, above caption zone). Assets matched by filename words to when they are spoken. Look: warm off-white paper, faint blue grid, film grain, boiling black felt-tip ink, one burnt-red accent; hard cut in with a click and brief exposure flash.
- Export: H.264 High L4.1, ≤6 Mbps, BT.709 tags, 48 kHz stereo AAC, no metadata/chapters/edit lists, faststart; validation before posting.
- Revisions: plain-language note → Claude allowed ops only (fix caption words, edit/remove/add cutaway, cut spoken words, change music, change filter preset, change subtitle); every op validated (real words/cutaways, cutaway rules, never remove more than half); impossible requests explained, nothing changes; previous edit backed up; Undo; "what changed" list.
- Post captions: hook line + one or two plain sentences in the creator's voice; no hashtags from Claude, creator's fixed set appended as last line; no em or en dashes (converted to commas); ~25 AI-tell phrases checked, one rewrite if found.
- Claude calls: plan cutaways (medium effort, structured output), subtitle (low), post caption (low), revision plan (medium, structured output).
- Settings: filter sliders with live preview, posting slots + timezone, socials and TikTok comment/duet/stitch permissions, music level (-39 LUFS), vocabulary, handles per platform with misheard-handle correction, processing Gentle/Fast (per-tenant concurrency), follow card on/off + cue, opening title on/off + series title, auto captions on/off + guidance + hashtag line, manual queueing.

Edge functions: create-upload (signed TUS), start-pipeline, trigger-callback (signed), request-revision, rewrite-caption, pass-video (assign slot), zernio-sync (cron), connect-social.
SQL: profiles, creator_settings, filter_presets, music_tracks, videos (status enum through the pipeline), video_assets, transcripts (words jsonb), edits (edit_json jsonb, version), edit_revisions, renders, cutaway_plans, post_captions, scheduled_posts (zernio ids, live links), social_accounts, job_events, usage_ledger, subscriptions, stripe_events; buckets raw-videos, assets, renders, music.
Sidebar: Videos, Review, Queue, Assets, then Documentation, Help, Billing, Settings.
Costs: usage = videos per customer per month (default 30).
Marketing: coaches, consultants, SaaS founders, agency owners, podcasters; "send me a raw clip, I send back an edited reel"; post your own Ethereel reels daily; comparison vs Opus Clip, Submagic, CapCut, Captions.ai, Descript.
