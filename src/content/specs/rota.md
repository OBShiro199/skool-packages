# Rota: Staff rotas built in minutes, with shift swaps and reminders by text.
slug `rota`, category `productivity`, art `{ motif: 'calendar', tone: 'amber' }`, status `full`, difficulty 2 or 3 (your call), buildHours your estimate, 3-letter id prefix of your choice (unique, not used by another kit). No price given: compute per-customer cost from researched API prices and set 2 to 3 plans at 75%+ gross margin at each plan's cap, monthly + annual (10x), 7-day trial; maths in costs.notes.

- Managers of cafes, gyms, salons, clinics set locations, roles, staff, availability and labour budget.
- Drag-and-drop weekly rota builder with templates, conflict and overtime warnings, cost per day; Claude suggests a draft rota from availability and last weeks (manager approves).
- Staff get a mobile web app: see shifts, set availability, request swaps or cover, accept open shifts; managers approve.
- SMS reminders (Twilio, A2P/UK sender ID notes) and email (Resend) for publish, changes, and 2 hours before shift.
- Clock-in via the mobile app with optional geofence, timesheet export (CSV for payroll).
- Multi-tenant: business workspaces with locations; RLS by workspace and location.
- Costs: usage = staff per business and SMS per month; plans per location at 75%+ margin.
- Marketing: hospitality and fitness owners with 5 to 50 staff.
- Sidebar: core pages, then Documentation, Help, Billing, Settings.
- Everything else per KIT_BRIEF.md.
