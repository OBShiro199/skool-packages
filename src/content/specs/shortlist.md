# Shortlist: Applicant tracking for small teams hiring fewer than ten people a year.
slug `shortlist`, category `business`, art `{ motif: 'kanban', tone: 'blue' }`, status `full`, difficulty 2 or 3 (your call), buildHours your estimate, 3-letter id prefix of your choice (unique, not used by another kit). - Create a job, get a hosted careers page and application form (custom questions, CV upload to private Storage).
- Pipeline per job (applied, screen, interview, offer, hired, rejected) with drag and drop, scorecards, notes, @mentions, team invites (workspaces, roles: owner/admin/interviewer).
- Claude (`claude-haiku-5-5`) summarises CVs against the job criteria with a transparent rubric (never auto-rejects; human decides; bias guardrails written into the PRD).
- Scheduling: interviewer availability via Google Calendar API, candidate self-books a slot.
- Templated emails to candidates via Resend (received, interview invite, rejection), GDPR: retention period + candidate data deletion requests.
- Costs: usage = applicants processed per month; plans at 75%+ margin.
- Marketing: startups and agencies hiring their first 10 people.
- Sidebar: core pages, then Documentation, Help, Billing, Settings.
